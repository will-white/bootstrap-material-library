Toolbars, button groups, split buttons

The M3 Expressive additions. Groups mark their selected member with aria-pressed or a hidden .m3-btn-check; the split button's toggle opens a native popover menu. Bootstrap .btn-group markup renders as a connected group. The dense bar below is the editor-toolbar shape (a slide or document editor's formatting row): one 40px row, 28px controls two pixels apart, hairlines between groups, compact native selects and popover menus, a stepper, and color swatches. Narrow the window and the low-priority groups fold away from the right while the overflow button appears.
